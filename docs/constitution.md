# Constitution

These are the enduring values of Auto-Guardable. They are intended to change only under exceptional circumstances.

## Non-Negotiable Values

- Protecting property begins with respecting people.
- Human dignity is a product requirement.
- De-escalation should be attempted before escalation whenever safety permits.
- Privacy is the default, not a premium feature.
- Transparency builds trust.
- Automated decisions affecting people should be explainable.
- Local operation is preferred whenever practical.
- The architecture should preserve user freedom rather than create dependency.
- Technology exists to assist people, not diminish them.

## Purpose

Auto-Guardable exists to demonstrate that protecting people and property does not require sacrificing dignity, privacy, or transparency. We believe the most effective security systems are those that resolve situations with the least necessary conflict while remaining accountable to the people they serve.
